# Code of Conduct

Hopper Files welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Participation in issues, discussions, pull requests, and reviews should make technical collaboration possible.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and behavior respectfully.
- Give actionable feedback and allow others to clarify or correct mistakes.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and other people's work.
- Keep discussion relevant and disclose conflicts of interest when they affect a contribution.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, disclosure of private information, and persistent unwanted contact are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

The maintainer may request changes, remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or close a discussion when needed to enforce these expectations. Responses should reflect the conduct and its impact.

For a concern involving public content, an issue may link to that interaction without republishing harmful or private material. Do not post sensitive incident reports in public issues. Use [GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for private reports of abuse or violations of GitHub's policies.

This project does not currently provide a separate private reporting mailbox.
